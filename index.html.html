Introduction

The Intelligent Campus Management System is a centralized software platform designed to manage various campus services such as energy management, food planning, security, library services, transportation routes, examinations, and club activities. It integrates these services into a single system to improve coordination, reduce inefficiency, and support better planning and decision-making.

Literature Review

Paper Title Author Main Idea Pros Cons
Unpacking Smart Campus Assessment (2024) Polin et al Develops an assessment framework to evaluate smart campus systems. Clear evaluation framework, structured indicators, useful for benchmarking. More conceptual; lacks full system implementation details.
The Making of Smart Campus: A Review and Conceptual Framework Muhammad et al Provides a structured architecture for integrating campus systems. Strong theoretical foundation, clear integration model. Limited real-world validation.
Smart Campus Frameworks and Technologies Review Min-Allah & Alrashed Reviews technologies like IoT, AI, and cloud in smart campuses. Covers multiple technologies, identifies challenges. Mostly theoretical, lacks implementation.
OnCampus Mobile Platform Bdiwi et al. Introduces a mobile-based campus management system. Improves interaction, real-time usability, better communication. Limited to mobile features only.
AI-based Smart Campus Management Kose & Kose Uses AI for automation, security, and decision-making. Enhances automation, improves decision-making. Requires large data and high computation.

Problem Statement

  • Campuses manage services like energy, food planning, security, library, transportation, examinations, and club activities using separate or manual systems.
  • This leads to inefficiency, poor coordination, data inconsistency, and delays in operations.
  • Therefore, a unified Intelligent Campus Management System is required to integrate these services into a single reliable and efficient platform.

Literature Review Summary

Analyzed 5+ research papers on smart campus systems, identifying gaps in integration, real-world validation, and scalability.

Research Gap / Innovation

Existing systems are fragmented, platform-specific, or purely theoretical. Our approach provides a unified, modular, and scalable integration model.
